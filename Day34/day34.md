---

# What is Marketing Investigation?

Marketing investigation is the process of collecting and analyzing campaign data, customer feedback, marketing metrics, and behavioral evidence to identify why a campaign succeeded or failed.

Rather than relying on assumptions, marketers use measurable evidence to discover the real cause of business outcomes.

---

# Why Evidence-Based Marketing Matters

Evidence-based marketing helps businesses make better decisions using data instead of intuition.

Benefits include:

- Better audience targeting
- Higher conversion rates
- Improved customer satisfaction
- More effective marketing campaigns
- Smarter budget allocation

# My Experience

The Marketing Detective simulation was engaging because it required analyzing campaign evidence before making a decision. Instead of guessing, I had to connect campaign metrics, customer feedback, and marketing performance to identify the real problem.

---

# Biggest Insight

My biggest insight was that campaign performance cannot be judged using a single metric. High reach or high engagement alone does not guarantee success—customer feedback, conversion data, and overall business outcomes must all be considered together.

---

# What I Learned

- Marketing decisions should be based on evidence.
- Customer feedback often reveals hidden campaign problems.
- Campaign metrics should be interpreted together, not individually.
- Budget allocation influences campaign performance.
- Strong engagement does not always lead to conversions.
- Identifying the root cause is essential before making improvements.
- Data-driven marketing leads to better strategic decisions.
- Continuous analysis helps optimize future campaigns.
