# Day 36 – Cognitive Pattern Explorer

## 🧠 Self-Reflection Journey

---

# Overview

On Day 36 of the #60DayClaudeChallenge, I built and explored the **Cognitive Pattern Explorer**, an interactive self-reflection application that encourages users to explore their thinking tendencies through everyday scenarios, prioritization exercises, and decision-making activities.

Unlike traditional personality quizzes, the experience focuses on reflection and learning rather than assessment. The generated insights are educational and should not be interpreted as clinical evaluations.

---

# What is Cognitive Pattern Explorer?

Cognitive Pattern Explorer is an offline educational web application that helps users understand how they naturally think and make decisions by completing interactive scenarios and activities.

The application emphasizes exploration and reflection rather than assigning fixed labels.

---

# Purpose of the Application

The purpose of this application is to:

- Explore personal thinking tendencies.
- Encourage thoughtful self-reflection.
- Demonstrate that thinking patterns vary depending on context.
- Teach through interactive experiences rather than static questionnaires.

---

# Why Self-Reflection Matters

Self-reflection helps us better understand how we approach decisions, solve problems, and respond to different situations. Recognizing our thinking habits can support more mindful decision-making and continuous personal growth.
