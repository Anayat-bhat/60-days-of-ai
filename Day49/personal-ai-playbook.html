What is this?

Personal AI Playbook is a private, offline toolkit for turning the way you already use AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot) into reusable, structured systems — instead of re-typing context and instructions from scratch every time.

1
DashboardBrowse ready-made workflow templates, grouped by what you actually do — learning, coding, docs, job search, content.
2
Workflow templatesEach one has fill-in-the-blank variables, a live preview, best practices, and a one-click copy button — so you paste a finished, high-quality prompt into any AI tool.
3
Prompt BuilderAssemble a custom prompt from building blocks (role, objective, context, constraints…) when no template fits. Live preview updates as you go.
4
Loop BuilderTurn any one-shot prompt into a self-checking, iterative prompt by defining a goal, how to judge success, and when to stop.
5
Your librarySave, favorite, duplicate, and edit your own workflows. Everything is stored locally in this browser — nothing leaves your machine.

You can reopen this any time from the ❔ button in the sidebar.
