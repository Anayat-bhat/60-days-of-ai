Workflow Summary

Scope a single believable AI feature, wireframe and design it, build a React + Tailwind frontend backed by a FastAPI service that calls an LLM or a trained model, test it end‑to‑end, deploy frontend and backend for free, then package the whole thing into a story: README, demo video, and a LinkedIn post. The project isn't done until someone outside your head can use it and understand why it exists in under 30 seconds.
