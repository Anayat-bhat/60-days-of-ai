# What is Media Literacy?

Media literacy is the ability to access, analyze, evaluate, and interpret information from different media sources critically instead of accepting it at face value.

It helps people distinguish factual information from misleading, biased, or emotionally manipulative content.

---

# Why Media Literacy Matters

Media literacy is important because headlines, social media posts, videos, and online articles can influence opinions through exaggeration or emotional language.

Being media literate allows us to:

- Think critically
- Verify information
- Recognize manipulation
- Avoid misinformation
- Make informed decisions before sharing content

# My Experience

The simulator demonstrated how easily headlines and emotional language can influence perception before we even read the full content.

Instead of testing existing knowledge, it encouraged careful observation, helping me recognize exaggeration, emotional triggers, and misleading framing in everyday media.

---

# Biggest Insight

My biggest insight was that emotional reactions often happen before logical thinking. Taking a moment to pause, question the wording, and compare the headline with the actual content helps prevent misinformation from spreading.

---

# What I Learned

- Headlines can exaggerate facts to attract attention.
- Emotional language is often used to increase engagement.
- Reading beyond the headline improves understanding.
- Critical thinking is essential before sharing information.
- Neutral language usually provides a clearer representation of facts.
- Media literacy helps identify manipulation techniques.
- Evaluating sources improves decision-making.
- Slowing down before reacting leads to more informed conclusions.
